Tenancy and access

  • Multi-tenant

    Each college is its own tenant; a platform console manages institutions.

  • Role-based access

    Student, faculty, parent, admin and platform roles, scoped by design.

  • Login and 2FA

    Email login, with optional two-factor available for staff accounts.

  • Onboarding

    Tenant setup, college settings and batch provisioning at the start of a term.

Integration

  • ERP sync and export

    Hooks for syncing, exporting and provisioning against your existing ERP.

  • LTI

    Connect external tools and existing learning content.

  • Integrations config

    Administered from the college console rather than by a support ticket.

  • Semester promotion

    Bulk promotion between semesters instead of manual re-enrollment.

What we will not claim

It matters more that this list is accurate than that it is long.

  • No invented certifications

    We do not list compliance badges we have not been audited for.

  • Data residency

    A real question that deserves a real answer from our engineering team.

  • Retention and deletion

    We will walk your team through the actual behaviour, not a summary.

  • Penetration testing

    Ask us directly about current status rather than reading it here.
