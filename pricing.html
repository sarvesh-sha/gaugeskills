Being straight about it

Why the number is not on this page

Why is there no price on this page?

Because a number without your student count, department mix and integration needs would be a guess, and you would find out it was wrong at the worst possible moment. We would rather size it after a pilot shows real usage.

What drives the number?

Enrolled students in scope, how many departments are included, and whether you need ERP or LTI integration work. Nothing else.

Is the pilot free?

The pilot is scoped per department and priced to be small. Ask us for the current figure it depends on how much setup and onboarding support you want from our side.
