Privacy policy

Last updated August 2026

Template notice. This document is a starting point written for a product team, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by counsel familiar with Indian data protection law and your institutional contracts before publishing.

Who controls the data

When a college uses GaugeSkills, the college is the controller of student, faculty and parent records. GaugeSkills processes that data on the college's instructions under a written agreement. If you are a student or parent with a question about your record, your college is the right first point of contact.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, role and institutional affiliation.
  • Academic activity: enrollment, course progress, quiz attempts and attendance.
  • AI tutor interactions, which are stored so students can revise from them.
  • Technical logs needed to keep the service running and secure.

What we do with it

Data is used to deliver the platform: showing progress, generating practice material, producing the analytics your college has enabled, and supporting your users. We do not sell personal data and we do not use student records to advertise to anyone.

Tenancy and access

Each institution is a separate tenant. Access within a tenant is role-based: parents see only linked children, faculty see their own classes, and administrative views are limited to authorised staff. Staff accounts can be required to use two-factor login.

Retention, residency and deletion

Retention periods and hosting location are set out in the agreement with your institution. Rather than summarise them loosely here, we will confirm the current specifics in writing on request write to hello@gaugeskills.com.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data. Requests from students and parents are routed through the institution that holds the record, so that the college can verify identity and enrollment first.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@gaugeskills.com.
