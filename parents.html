For parents

Their child. Nothing else.

Parent accounts are scoped to linked children by design. There is no directory to browse, no other student's marks to see, and no phone call to the office required.

Book a pilot Full capability list

What a parent sees

  • Linked children only

    Access is scoped at the account level, not hidden by a filter.

  • Attendance

    The same record faculty marked, without a follow-up call.

  • Academic progress

    Course progress and where their child is struggling.

  • Exam performance

    Results and the report card as it develops.

Why colleges enable it

  • Fewer office visits

    Routine questions answer themselves.

  • Earlier intervention

    A parent who sees week 3 can help in week 3.

  • Advisor view

    Structured guidance rather than raw numbers without context.

  • One source of truth

    The college, the parent and the student see the same record.

Parent visibility is optional and controlled by the college. It can be switched on department by department.
