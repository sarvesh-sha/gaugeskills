Scope

Accounts

Platform accounts are issued by your institution. Keep your credentials to yourself, use two-factor login where your college has enabled it, and tell your administrator promptly if you believe an account has been compromised.

Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to access records belonging to other students or staff.

AI-generated content

The platform generates teaching material, quizzes and tutoring responses. Generated output is a draft: faculty review and approve material before it reaches students, and tutor responses can contain errors. Treat them as study support rather than an authoritative source, and follow your institution's guidance on academic integrity.
