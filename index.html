The gap

The gap is not content. It is help after class.

Ask any HOD where the semester goes wrong and you get a version of these three answers.

  • Doubt waits

    A student stuck at 11pm waits until the next class, or quietly gives up. The stretch between the lecture and the exam is where drop-off actually happens.

  • Faculty cannot scale

    Personal notes, fresh quizzes and follow-ups do not survive sections of 60 to 120 students. The intent is there. The hours are not.

  • Signals arrive late

    Most colleges discover the problem in the result sheet. By the time the marks are out, the semester is already gone and the backlog is real.

Explain Newton's second law with a daily-life example in simple English.

AI

Newton's second law says force equals mass times acceleration F = ma. Think about pushing a scooter versus pushing a loaded truck with the same effort. The scooter picks up speed quickly because its mass is small. The truck barely moves, because the same force spread over a much larger mass produces far less acceleration. So for a fixed force, heavier means slower to speed up.

Straight answers

The questions that always come up

If we do not know an answer, we will book the follow-up rather than guess on a website.

Will faculty actually use it?

Start with one teacher, not a mandate. The content studio saves them a Sunday, and that is the hook. Faculty keep authorship the AI produces a draft that the teacher edits and approves before anything reaches students.

Isn’t coverage tracking just surveillance?

Faculty own the plan they set. Leadership sees the gap between planned and delivered, and the intended first response is support an extra session, a tutorial, a lab. We would rather you frame it that way with your staff from day one.

Is student data safe?

Each college is an isolated tenant, access is role-based, and staff accounts can require two-factor login. For questions about data residency, retention and audits, we will put our engineering team in a call with your IT team rather than answer loosely on a website.

What about low bandwidth?

The platform installs as a PWA and is built phone-first, so the interface is light and works on a modest connection. Voice and AI generation do need connectivity we would rather be straight about that than promise offline AI.

Does it replace our existing LMS?

It does not have to. GuageSkill is a complete LMS on its own, but it also connects over LTI, so colleges that are committed to Moodle or Classroom can add the tutor and the analytics layer on top.

What does it cost?

A pilot is scoped per department. Licensing after that is per student per year, priced once we can both see real usage from the pilot. Request a quote and we will size it against your actual student numbers.
