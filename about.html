About us

Built around one question a principal cannot answer today.

Is the syllabus actually being covered, and which students are quietly falling behind while it is? Every part of GaugeSkills exists to make that answerable on a Tuesday rather than at the end of term.

What we believe

Four things we designed around

  • Content was never the bottleneck

    Every syllabus is already documented and every textbook already exists. What a student cannot get at 11pm is a patient explanation of the thing they did not follow in class.

  • Faculty are not the problem to solve

    The intent is there. The hours are not. Anything that adds work to a teacher's week fails, no matter how good the demo looked.

  • A signal in week 15 is not a signal

    Result sheets are a historical record. Useful information arrives early enough that somebody can still do something about it.

  • Measurement should serve support

    Coverage and performance data exist so a struggling section gets an extra lab, not so a department can rank its teachers.
