Platform overview

A complete LMS, with a measurement layer on top.

GuageSkill runs the day-to-day operations of a department courses, attendance, homework, exams and adds the two things a traditional LMS was never built to do: teach a student one to one, and tell leadership what is actually happening.

Book a pilot Full capability list

Teach and learn

The everyday surface students and faculty live in.

  • Course structure

    Stream, course, chapter and module the shape Indian programmes already use.

  • AI tutor and voice

    Conversational help with saved sessions, so it becomes revision material.

  • Quizzes and prep

    Generated practice sets with mastery tracking per chapter.

  • Live and recorded classes

    Teaching stays in the same place as the material.

  • Homework and assignments

    Assessment sits in the same loop as learning.

  • Discussions and announcements

    Class communication without a parallel WhatsApp group.

Run the department

The operational record the exam cell and office depend on.

  • Smart attendance

    Faculty mark it once; students and parents both see it.

  • Exams and sections

    Assessment scheduling alongside section management.

  • Curriculum pacing

    Plan the semester week by week against the approved syllabus.

  • Report card

    A single academic record for the student and the parent.

  • Messages and directory

    Inbox, sent and a searchable people directory.

  • Notifications

    Announcements that actually reach the class.

Measure and predict

The layer that turns activity into a decision.

  • Performance analyzer

    Chapter-level success across a class, not just an average.

  • Predicted grade

    A forward signal before the exam rather than after it.

  • At-risk flags

    A shortlist short enough to act on.

  • Teaching snapshot

    Courses handled, classes conducted, coverage against plan.

  • Faculty evaluation

    Structured evaluation in one place.

  • Admin analytics and AI insights

    College-wide rollups for leadership.

Every capability listed here exists in the product today. If something you need is missing, we would rather tell you that in the first call than discover it during rollout.
